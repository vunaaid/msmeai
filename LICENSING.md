## What is a "Real User"?

A **natural person** with login credentials to the system.

**AI agents, bots, service accounts, cron jobs, and automated workers do NOT
count.** Run as many agents as you like — only real people are counted.

## Honor system (no technical enforcement)

The software has **no license key, no activation, no
metering, and never blocks features**. Even past 4 users, the app keeps working.

We trust your honesty. If your organization exceeds the free tier, please buy a
license to support the project and stay compliant. Using beyond the free tier
without a commercial license is still a **breach of the license**, even though
nothing technically stops you.
